## Convoy cohesion

Native convoy vehicles are tagged from the actual Ultimate AIVehInit context; debug vehicles and our supplemental escorts join the same cohesion controller. Routes and native mission scripts remain in control. Every4seconds the controller assesses route progress and actual separation, slows the front and holds it when intact stragglers are too far behind, targeting approximately300-400m overall cohesion with35m nominal separation. It resumes as they catch up. Dead/immobile/driverless, captured and player-occupied vehicles are excluded. Recently attacked convoys are not held stationary by this system in an ambush.

Stalled AI receive their current navigation order again. After60seconds without progress, a maximum of three nearby clear-road reposition attempts is allowed, only outside player proximity/line of sight. There is no repair, resurrection or player-vehicle teleport. It does not promise perfect driving on every mod map: Arma road graphs, bends, bridges, wrecks, AI combat and ownership changes still matter. Missing-waypoint fallback uses truck heading instead of the map origin. Owner-executed pace/recovery/restore requests pass through a server-origin-only RPC, including HC-owned vehicles.

## Industrial contamination response

The existing three native CWP bombs, native cloud,0.5-second watchdog and JIP behavior remain. **Industrial** isotope truck destruction additionally dispatches a response. Medical isotope trucks still release their existing cloud but do not dispatch this industrial-only hunt.

Actual convoy attackers are recorded by playerUID and hit time; recent attackers (last10minutes, including the killer) are eligible. Nearby innocent players are not selected. Unattributed Zeus deletion/AI accidents do not manufacture a culprit. Repeated industrial wrecks cannot stack duplicate aerial responses on overlapping targets.

- One installed **RKSL Watchkeeper WK450**, discovered from the loaded configuration. A protected RKSL PBO could not be extracted; no attempt was made to bypass protection. Runtime discovery verified `rksla3_uav_watchkeeper_uk`.
- It patrols up to **60minutes**, or stops surveillance if destroyed, its pilot is lost, or it is captured/player-controlled. **No replacement drone**, per the user's explicit answer. Reports occur every20seconds when an eligible target is within3km and has geometric line of sight. The drone is unarmed. Existing native visual/IR settings remain.
- Contacts are shared to all currently spawned hostile occupant/invader AI groups across the map, with mobile groups receiving a hunt waypoint. Static guns receive knowledge; protected convoy and response-flight groups retain their navigation. This cannot make Antistasi's virtual/unspawned garrisons physically attack or create infinite map-wide reinforcements. No global faction hostility changes are used.
- One full24-seat **3CB MV-22 Osprey**, preferring `UK3CB_B_Osprey_USMC_WD`, delivers actual **NATO Woodland CBRN Specialists** discovered as `B_W_Soldier_CBRN_F`. Their existing NATO woodland appearance is retained, but units join the convoy owner's already-hostile side, avoiding friendly-fire/faction rewrites. Flight crew are appropriate pilots; every passenger is a specialist.
- A flat clear LZ is sought650-1100m from the target. A real VTOL approach and `landAt` disembark is used. No midair forced disembark or teleport onto players. Failed insertions log diagnostics. Aircraft departs after unloading. Ground specialists hunt last-reported positions and their own AI contacts; shooting down the drone stops fresh shared reports, not already-landed infantry.
- The controller ends at its hour deadline or when no eligible target remains alive/online. Cleanup now retries every30seconds after response completion, removing its own distant assets once players leave. It preserves captured/player-controlled vehicles, captives and unrelated occupants; own units aboard an unrelated vehicle are also released. Dedicated geometry checks do not simulate every weather/thermal detection nuance.
